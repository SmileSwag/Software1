================================================================
                         HOW TO PLAY
================================================================
1. GAMEPLAY & SCORE:
   • You have 100 moves total.
   • Step on Gold: +3 Points (Respawns at a new spot!)
   • Step on Mine: -1 Point  (Respawns at a new spot!)

2. CONTROLS:
   Use WASD keys to move across the 5x5 grid:
   [W] Up | [S] Down | [A] Left | [D] Right | [M] Menu

3. DIFFICULTY MODES:
   • [1] Easy:   1 Mine  | 1 Gold
   • [2] Medium: 2 Mines | 1 Gold
   • [3] Hard:   3 Mines | 1 Gold

4. DETECTION EQUIPMENT:
   • Metal Detector: Warns [MINE NEARBY!] if a mine is within 3x3.
   • Gold Radar: Reports [WARMER] or [COLDER] based on distance.
